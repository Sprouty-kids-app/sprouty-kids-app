Privacy Policy

Twigty – learning games for kids · Effective date: 18 November 2026

In short: Twigty does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the child does in the app stays on the device.

Who we are

Twigty is made by ARTEVIA spol. s r.o., Prague, Czech Republic, company ID (IČO) 27658813 (“we”). Contact: hello@sprouty-kids.com.

Information we collect

None. Twigty does not ask for, collect or send names, e-mail addresses, photos, voice recordings, location, device identifiers or any other personal information. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

What is stored on the device

To keep the child's progress, the app saves a few things only on the device: collected stars, bought stickers, finished pictures in “My Art”, today's tasks, the sound setting and whether Premium is unlocked. This information never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is deleted. Parents can also clear it in For parents → Reset progress.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Twigty shows no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. We do not share or sell any information to anyone.

Purchases and subscriptions

Twigty Premium can be bought as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or receive payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in the device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

To know whether Premium is active (for example after a renewal or a cancellation), the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a service that checks App Store purchases. RevenueCat receives only a random, anonymous ID created by the app and the purchase information from the App Store — no name, e-mail address or other personal information, and nothing about how the child uses the app. It is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Parental gate

Purchases, links that open outside the app and the parents' settings are protected by a parental gate (a short sum for grown-ups), so children cannot reach them on their own.

Children's privacy

Twigty is designed for children aged 2–6. Because we collect no personal information, no information about children is gathered, stored or shared. We follow the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's guidelines for the Kids category.

Your rights

As we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about privacy, write to us at hello@sprouty-kids.com. You also have the right to contact the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
