Made for little ones who can't read yet

Twigty speaks every instruction, cheers every success and gently helps after a mistake. Big buttons, bright pictures and no confusing menus.

  • Every task and word is spoken aloud
  • Twigty cheers, waves, and even gets sleepy at bedtime
  • No time pressure, no failing, just trying again
  • Works great on iPhone and iPad, even offline

Earn stars, collect stickers

About us

Twigty is made by ARTEVIA spol. s r.o., a company based in Prague, Czech Republic.

ARTEVIA develops and publishes Twigty, an educational app for children aged 2–6. We design the activities, illustrations and voice recordings with one goal: a calm, safe and joyful first step into learning.

Twigty is available for iPhone and iPad. Parents can try it for free and unlock all content with Twigty Premium. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store.
