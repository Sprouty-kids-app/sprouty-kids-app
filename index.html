Sprouty

Learn · Play · Grow — learning games for kids aged 2–6

Coming soon to the App Store

Sprouty is a friendly little sprout who helps young children learn through play. Every activity is spoken aloud, so children who cannot read yet can play on their own.

Made with care for families: no ads, no tracking, and purchases protected by a parental gate.

What children can do

NumbersCount from 1 to 100, how many, which is more
First Words12 picture categories with words and animal sounds
Colouring62 pictures, glitter, patterns and stickers
PuzzlesReal jigsaw pieces from 2 to 16
Shapes & MathShapes, adding, taking away, missing numbers
GamesMemory, Simon, Word Match, Animal Sounds and more

Our promise to parents

  • No advertising and no tracking
  • No personal information collected — progress stays on the device
  • Purchases, links and settings behind a grown-ups-only question
